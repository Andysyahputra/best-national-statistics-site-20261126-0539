Sources & Method

Every figure on this site comes from one of two publishers: the World Bank (World Development Indicators) for population and GDP, and BPS — Statistics Indonesia for growth, inflation, jobs, poverty, life expectancy, sectors and provinces. Each entry below names the figure, its source, its year and a short note. Province shares are the site’s own arithmetic: province population divided by the national total of 283.5 million. Sector shares are labelled “approx.” because BPS publishes them rounded.

    Method notes

    • Population, GDP and GDP per capita are World Bank figures for 2024.
    • GDP growth is real (inflation-adjusted) growth, year on year, from BPS.
    • Inflation is the Consumer Price Index, year on year, measured each December, from BPS.
    • Unemployment is the open unemployment rate for February 2025, from BPS.
    • Poverty is the share of the population below the poverty line, March 2025, from BPS.
    • The province table lists the 18 most populous of Indonesia’s 38 provinces; the remaining 20 provinces total about 19.5 million people.
    • Nothing on this site is estimated by the site itself, except the province shares, which are simple divisions of the published figures.
