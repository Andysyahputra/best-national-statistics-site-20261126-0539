Sources & Method

Method

Shares. Each province's share is its population divided by the 283.5 million national total, shown to two decimal places.

Change in percentage points. The growth chart's “Change (pp)” view subtracts the previous year's growth rate from the current year's; 2020 has no previous year shown, so it reads “n/a”.

Rounding. Figures are shown exactly as published; sector shares are approximate and labelled as such.

Currency. Dollar figures are in current US dollars as published by the World Bank.

Freshness. Data as of November 2026. When BPS or the World Bank publish new figures, this page is where the update will be noted first.
