About this site

Nusantara Numbers gathers Indonesia’s headline statistics in one place, in plain language, with every figure labelled with its source and year. The Economy page covers output, prices and the sectors behind GDP. The People page covers population, jobs, poverty and how long Indonesians live. The Provinces page ranks the 18 most populous provinces. The Sources page lists every figure used here, with its source, year and a short note — and the raw data files behind the site, free to download.

All figures are as published by the World Bank and by BPS (Statistics Indonesia). Nothing here is estimated by this site.
