About this site

Nusantara Numbers is a small, fast reference for Indonesia's national statistics. It uses only two sources — the World Bank and BPS (Statistics Indonesia) — and every figure on every page carries its source and year.

Go deeper: the Economy page has growth, inflation and sector shares; People has the population trend, poverty and life expectancy; Provinces ranks all 18 most populous provinces; Sources & Method lists every figure and lets you download the data.
